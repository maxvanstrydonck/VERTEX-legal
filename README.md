# VERTEX — Support

VERTEX is a workout tracker with a social feed, for iPhone.

## Get help

For any question, bug report, account issue, or data request, email
**support@vertexstrengthlab.com**. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

## Report content or users

You can report an athlete from their profile page inside the app, or a specific
post from the feed, and block any athlete (your block list is in Settings).
Names, captions and comments are also screened automatically against a list of
prohibited terms. Reports are aimed to be reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.

## Delete your account

In the app: **Settings → Danger zone → Delete account**. This permanently
deletes your account, profile, workouts, and photos from our servers.
